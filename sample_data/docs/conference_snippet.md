## Weekend series

A Region Z weekend series is three games, Friday through Sunday. Friday is
nine innings. Saturday and Sunday are nine innings unless both coaches agree
to seven before first pitch on that day. A weekday midweek game is not part
of the weekend series and does not count toward the three-game set.

## Roster listing

The handbook requires a 28-player active list submitted by 5:00 p.m. local
time the day before the first game of a series. A player who is not on that
list may not appear. This excerpt does not name eligibility rules, medical
status, or any real school.

## Travel

Home series have no required arrival window. For a road series the visiting
club is asked to arrive in town at least four hours before first pitch on
Friday. That is a handbook request, not a measured travel time from any
schedule file.
