# How this staff communicates

Synthetic. Written for the sample data in this repo, not for any real program.

## To players

Short. One thing to work on, not five. Say what you saw, say what it costs,
say what you want instead. Never compare one player to another by name.

## To parents

Plain language, no jargon, no numbers unless the number is the point. Say who
to contact and by when. Playing time is a conversation with the coach, not a
line in a group message. Never discuss another family's kid.

## To staff

Numbers first, then the recommendation, then what you need from them. If a
number is soft, say the sample size out loud.

## Standing rules

- Nothing goes out over the staff's name without a coach reading it first.
- Injury and eligibility details stay verbal. Do not put them in writing in a
  system, and do not repeat them to anyone outside the staff.
- Report numbers are for preparation. Do not quote a scouting number to a
  player about himself in a group setting.
- If the data is thin, say so in the same sentence as the number.
